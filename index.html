The Insight

Technology has never been the problem. The relationship has.

Every AI platform in the world can generate text. Process requests. Answer questions. Almost none of them can maintain a real relationship across time — one that remembers, adapts, and deepens.

MyBFF Coach was built from a different premise entirely. The PTSD framing on Coach Jeff is the door. The recovery support on Coach Recovery is the door. The relationship — the persistent, trust-first, human-centered connection — is the product.

That's what separates this platform from everything else being built.

Persistent Memory

Every interaction builds on the last. The platform doesn't reset. It remembers what matters — and you can delete it anytime. Understanding compounds over weeks, months, years.

Real Voice. Real Presence.

Each coach has a distinct voice that feels human. Not a robot reading a script. A presence you recognize and return to.

Behavioral Intelligence

Built on the science of influence, trust, and human behavior change. Adapts to where people are — not where the system thinks they should be.

Safety by Design

Crisis awareness, boundary respect, escalation pathways — structural. Not a feature toggle. Because the people using this deserve that standard.

Founder Story

Rusty Humphries is a Gold Star son. His father, Captain Gary D. Humphries, Executive Officer of the 229th Assault Helicopter Battalion, 1st Cavalry Division, was killed in a rocket attack at Dau Tieng, Vietnam, on January 26, 1969. His name is on the Wall, Panel 34W, Line 84. Rusty spent 15 years hosting the nationally syndicated Rusty Humphries Show on more than 250 stations, and made veterans and active-duty issues central to it. He traveled to Iraq, Afghanistan, and Guantanamo Bay to support deployed forces, and served as national co-spokesman for the Vietnam Veterans Memorial Fund's Education Center at The Wall campaign. He built Coach Jeff to be the buddy his father's generation never had.
